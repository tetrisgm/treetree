Lyon, 10 September 1986

Dear Nora,

Your father Leo and I have finished unpacking in Lyon. I put the recipe
notebook beside the kitchen window. Please tell your brother Owen that
there is room for his little painted boat on the shelf.

I turned thirty-three this year. Your grandmother Rosa says she still
remembers the warm Lisbon spring when I was born.

Love,
Maya Rowan
